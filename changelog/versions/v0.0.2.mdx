## Welcome

Obviously, there's a lot thats changed if you look through the docs. First and foremost, the name has changed from "Starskiff" to "Raggy." 
I've also been changing "repos" to "projects" as that just makes more sense. I've also realized that I didnt really make this a proper
changelog, so I'll also be fixing it as I go. These docs may be out of date for a couple days, and I apologize for the inconvenience.

## Main Changes

In a quick and orderly fasion

- Rebrand to "Raggy.dev"
- Modified it so there is no "sharing" like I had originally made it. Everything is private and your data is yours.
- Implemented connections: upload data from google drive. More connections comming soon.
- Quality of life updates: The ingestion pipeline has improved significantly. Still lots of work to be done on it, and more improvements to come.

## Next Up

I plan to implement a way to get your users' data, similar to how Carbon AI did. I also want to update more on the ingestion pipeline as well as adding RAG on images and videos.
I want to expand to give you more Vector Database options, and add LLMs to the output response. The reason I havent was because I wanted to make it easy to plug into any workflow.
I thought the best way to do that, would be to make it so you can plug your data into any LLM of your choosing, and not restrict you to LLMs Raggy provides.
